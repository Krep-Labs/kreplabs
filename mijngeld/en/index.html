What it looks like

Everything you need, nothing more

Your balance and the forecast

See what's in your account now and what you'll have left at the end of the month, including the recurring costs still to come.

Import from your bank

Bring in your transactions from ING, Rabobank, ABN AMRO or bunq. The app picks the category and skips duplicates.

Recurring costs

Rent, subscriptions and salary come back every month. You confirm them with one tap.

Budgets and savings goals

Set a budget per category and save up for something. You see how close you are.

Multiple accounts

Checking and savings neatly apart, with transfers between them.

Insights per month and per year

Where did your money go, what came in, and how much did you save this year?

Reminders and widget

A nudge at the right moment and your balance on your home screen.

Private and secure

No ads and no trackers. PIN, fingerprint and two-step verification.

Your money is yours

Mijn Geld has no ads and doesn't track you. Your data is only used to make the app work, and you can delete your account and everything in it yourself.
